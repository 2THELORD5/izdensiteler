02 / Projects

Nine garden directions

Scroll through the portfolio — each landscape moves from daylight into night.

  • 01 Wadi House

    Al Barari, Dubai

    A warm limestone garden organized around mature olive trees, generous terraces and a calm swimming pool.

  • 02 Ember Court

    Jumeirah, Dubai

    Dark basalt paving, corten planters and a linear water channel create a precise garden with a strong architectural character.

  • 03 Sienna Dunes

    Dubai Hills, Dubai

    A water-wise desert landscape combining pale sandstone, sculptural succulents, gravel gardens and a dark reflecting basin.

  • 04 Palm Veil

    Emirates Hills, Dubai

    A tropical-modern garden where palms, textured concrete and a slim turquoise pool form a private outdoor retreat.

  • 05 Kanso Court

    Nad Al Sheba, Dubai

  • 06 Cypress Line

  • 07 Sky Grove

  • 08 Azure Edge

    Hatta, Dubai

04 / Climate

Designed with Dubai,
not against it.

Heat, glare, water and wind are design inputs. We use them to shape landscapes that feel generous without asking too much of the environment.

  • Shade first

    Comfortable routes, outdoor rooms and thresholds planned around the movement of the sun.

  • Water with purpose

    Pools and reflecting water used for cooling, atmosphere and responsible enjoyment.

  • Materials that settle in

    Stone, timber and finishes selected to withstand Dubai’s climate and age with grace.
