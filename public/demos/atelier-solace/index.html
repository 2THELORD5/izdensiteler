Rituals

Three ways to slow down.

  1. Spring wash

    Hands, face and feet in cold spring water, then a pot of barley tea.

    30 min

  2. Stone bath

    A private stone bath at 39 °C, lit by a single window. Salt and cedar on request.

    50 min

  3. Warm-stone massage

    Basalt stones warmed in water, oil pressed along the back in long, slow lines.

    80 min
