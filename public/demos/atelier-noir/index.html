FAQ

How long does NOIR 01 last on skin?

Eight to ten hours on moisturised skin; projection softens after the second hour — by design.

Do you ship outside Turkey?

EU and UK via DHL Express. Duties calculated at checkout in the full boutique — this demo does not process orders.

Can I layer with another scent?

We do not recommend it. NOIR is composed as a closed arc.

Is the limited coffret refillable?

Travel atomiser yes; main flacon is sealed batch — return to the maison for engraving and recycle.
