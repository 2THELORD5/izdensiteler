The studio

The best rooms of the seventies never went out of style. They went out of production.

Retro Interior Company creates rooms with memory, color, and modern comfort. We restore the best ideas of the late sixties and seventies—then make them work beautifully now.

That means conversation pits that host real conversations, lacquer that survives daily life, and palettes with the confidence of a record sleeve. Nostalgic in reference, contemporary in everything you touch.

From first sketch to final styling, every project is built to be lived in—not looked at.
