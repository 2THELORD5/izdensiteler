Nightcap Bar

A late-night jewel box in oxblood lacquer, amber glass, and polished chrome.

Built for the last hour of the evening.

Small rooms make strong bars. Oxblood lacquer wraps walls and ceiling into one continuous glow, while the walnut counter curves to keep every seat in the conversation.

Amber glass shelving backlights the bottles like a stage, chrome stools catch the candlelight, and the checkered stone floor gives the whole scene its rhythm. It photographs dark and drinks warm.

Materials

  • Oxblood lacquer
  • Amber glass
  • Chrome
