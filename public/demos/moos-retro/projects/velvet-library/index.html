Velvet Library

A townhouse reading room made deep, quiet, and richly tactile.

Quiet is a material too.

The brief asked for a room that ends the day. Forest-green paneling and walnut shelving absorb the light; moss velvet and oxblood leather absorb everything else.

Brass picture lights are aimed at the shelves, not the ceiling, so the whole room reads by lamplight. It is designed for one hour and one chapter at a time.

Materials

  • Moss velvet
  • Walnut
  • Aged brass
