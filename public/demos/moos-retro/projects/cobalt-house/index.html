Cobalt House

Desert light sharpened by cobalt lacquer, chrome, and one precise hit of red.

One blue, taken seriously.

Desert houses drown easily in beige. Here a single storage wall in cobalt lacquer runs the length of the plan, giving the white plaster and travertine something to push against.

Chrome cantilever chairs and a red lacquered table supply the precise accents—nothing else competes. The palette is strict so the light can be generous.
