About the studio

Past perfect. Present tense.

We study how the era really lived—then design for how you live now.

Every project begins in research. We study the architecture, materials, furniture, and daily routines of the late sixties and seventies—the sunken lounges, the lacquered storage walls, the fearless palettes—and ask what made them feel so alive.

Then we translate. Modern comfort, current building standards, and the way you actually live shape every decision, so the finished room feels effortless rather than staged.

The result is never a museum set. It's your room, with a longer memory.

01

Principles

  1. Keep the bones

    Good architecture leads. We work with a building's character, never against it.

  2. Let color lead

    Palettes are decided early and carried through every material and finish.

  3. Make comfort current

    Period spirit, present-day performance—light, acoustics, and ease included.
