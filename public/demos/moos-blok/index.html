Architectural model making

Slide 1 of 5: Architectural model making. Built to be understood.

Physical models for projects that need to be seen clearly.

A model does more than reproduce a building. It reveals relationships—between form and site, public and private space, movement and landscape. We work with design teams from early studies through final presentation, selecting the right scale, material and level of detail for the decision at hand.

03

From file to finished model.

05 steps

  1. 01 / 05

    Step 1 of 5: Brief and quotation

    Send drawings, renderings, target scale, display purpose, deadline and delivery location. We review scope and return a clear proposal.

  2. 02 / 05

    Step 2 of 5: Model strategy

    We define the scale, crop, level of detail, materials, lighting zones, removable elements and display requirements.

  3. 03 / 05

    Step 3 of 5: Prototype and approval

    Critical façades, landscape textures, colours and lighting are sampled before full production.

  4. 04 / 05

    Step 4 of 5: Fabrication

    Components are laser-cut, CNC-milled or 3D-printed, then assembled and finished by hand.

  5. 05 / 05

    Step 5 of 5: Review and installation

    You receive structured progress updates. After final inspection, the model is packed, delivered and installed.
