Privacy notice

This notice explains what information Black Block collects through this website, why it is collected and how it is handled.

Draft for legal review. This document sets out the subjects Black Block intends to cover and must be reviewed and completed by a UAE-qualified legal professional, together with the confirmed business identity and contact details, before publication.

Who we are

Black Block is an architectural model-making studio based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

The registered business identity, licence details, postal address and contact channels are pending confirmation and will be stated here before this notice is published.

Information collected through the quote form

The project brief form asks for the following information:

  • Your name, company, work email and, optionally, a phone or WhatsApp number
  • Project name, location, purpose, model type, preferred scale or size
  • Required completion date and delivery or installation location
  • A written project description
  • Any files you choose to attach
  • Whether the project requires an NDA, and your consent to be contacted

File uploads and project confidentiality

Drawings, models and other files submitted with a brief are treated as confidential project material. Access is limited to the people involved in preparing your quotation and, if the project proceeds, producing the model.

Where a project requires an NDA, the terms of that agreement take precedence over this notice.

Why the information is used

Information submitted through the form is used to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quotation, ask follow-up questions and, where a project proceeds, deliver the work.

It is not used for unrelated marketing without a separate, explicit request from you.

Service providers and cross-border processing

Website hosting, email and file storage may involve service providers operating outside the United Arab Emirates. The specific providers, their locations and the safeguards applied will be listed here once confirmed.

How long information is kept

Enquiry records and project files are retained only for as long as needed for the purposes above and for any applicable record-keeping obligation. The confirmed retention periods will be stated here following legal review.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies and does not embed third-party tracking scripts. If analytics are introduced, this section will describe what is measured, the provider used and how to opt out.

Your rights and how to contact us

You may ask what information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted where we have no continuing need or obligation to keep it.

The contact route for these requests will be published here alongside the confirmed studio contact details.

Revisions

This notice is a draft pending legal review. The revision date will be recorded here when the reviewed version is published.
