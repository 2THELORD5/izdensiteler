Every model starts with its purpose.

Approval, competition, coordination, sales or exhibition—the purpose determines the scale, crop, detail, material and interaction. BB develops each model around that decision.

Discuss your model
01

What BB brings to the model.

Six decisions that shape the outcome, taken before anything is cut.

  • 01

    Scale and scope strategy

    We help select a scale that protects the important information. The result may be a complete site, a focused crop, a sectional model or a family of removable studies.

  • 02

    Lighting and interaction

    Programmable LED zones can demonstrate use, phasing, movement and day-to-night atmosphere. Controls remain simple, reliable and serviceable.

  • 03

    Material and colour matching

    Façade tone, glazing, landscape and ground finishes are sampled for approval. Materials are selected for appearance, stability and display life.

  • 04

    Modular construction

    Removable floors, roofs, plots and infrastructure layers let one model support several conversations.

  • 05

    Protection and delivery

    Every transport plan considers model geometry, vibration, climate, access routes, lifting and final placement.

  • 06

    Maintenance

    BB can provide cleaning, repairs, lighting checks, replacement covers and updates when a project changes.

02

Six model types.

Choose the type that matches the decision your model has to support.

Choose a model type

Building models

Single buildings and complex ensembles with accurate façades, structure, material character and site context.

Masterplan models

Large territories made readable through clear massing, terrain, mobility networks, landscape and programmable lighting.

Interior models

Sectional and removable-floor models that explain spatial sequence, fit-out, furniture and circulation.

Landscape models

Terrain, planting, water, shade and public realm represented with site-specific material and colour systems.

Industrial models

Facilities, logistics networks and technical processes clarified for planning, operations, safety and stakeholder review.

Exhibition models

Durable, illuminated centrepieces designed for sales galleries, exhibitions, museums and public presentations.

03

Production process.

Six steps from received files to a commissioned model in position.

06 steps

  1. 01 / 06

    Step 1 of 6: Review

    Drawings, BIM extracts, renders, specifications, model size, deadline and display purpose.

  2. 02 / 06

    Step 2 of 6: Resolve

    Scope, scale, crop, material language, lighting, removable elements, case and plinth.

  3. 03 / 06

    Step 3 of 6: Prototype

    Sample critical geometry, façade pattern, colour, landscape and light.

  4. 04 / 06

    Step 4 of 6: Fabricate

    Laser cut, CNC mill, 3D print, cast, paint and hand finish.

  5. 05 / 06

    Step 5 of 6: Assemble

    Integrate structure, model components, landscape, lighting and controls.

  6. 06 / 06

    Step 6 of 6: Inspect and install

    Complete quality review, pack, deliver, place and commission.

04

Technology in the studio.

Each process is chosen per component — geometry, tolerance and finish decide, not preference.

01Precision laser cutting

Clean, repeatable components for façades, screens, glazing frames, roads and fine graphic layers.

02High-resolution resin printing

Complex curvature, fine joints and small repeated components produced with consistent geometry.

03CNC terrain machining

Accurate topography, large bases and rigid structural components cut directly from coordinated digital files.

04Smart lighting

Zoned, dimmable and programmable illumination for programme, circulation, phasing and atmosphere.

05Responsible material selection

Recycled and lower-impact substrates used where performance allows, with waste-aware nesting and modular parts designed for replacement.

06Precision assembly

Controlled jigs, measured interfaces and service access protect alignment through transport and long-term display.
