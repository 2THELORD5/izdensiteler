We make architecture physical.

Black Block is a Dubai-based architectural model studio built around one belief: complex design becomes more useful when people can gather around it.

Visit the studio
01

One table. Many disciplines.

Model making sits between architecture, engineering, fabrication and visual communication. Our role is to hold those disciplines together—understanding the design intent, coordinating accurate geometry and deciding what the finished object must communicate.

BB combines digital production with the judgement of experienced makers. Machines give us repeatability; hands give the model its material character, alignment and final clarity.

We work in close review cycles. Clients see samples early, receive progress at agreed milestones and know which decisions are still open.

02

Four principles we work to.

They decide what gets built, what gets tested first and what gets protected.

  • 01

    Clarity before detail

    Detail is useful only when it helps someone understand the project.

  • 02

    Prototype the risk

    Test difficult geometry, finish and light before committing to full production.

  • 03

    Build for the display life

    Consider transport, cleaning, service access and future updates from the beginning.

  • 04

    Protect the project

    Treat drawings, pricing, competitions and launch plans as confidential information.

A connected production workflow.

File preparation, laser cutting, CNC machining, resin printing, hand assembly, surface finishing, electronics, cases, packing and installation are managed as one coordinated scope. Partner processes are documented and quality-controlled by BB.
