Sapa Residences rises from the east bank of Dubai Creek Harbour — a sculpted tower of curved balconies and layered gardens, positioned between the flamingo wetlands of Ras Al Khor and the icons of Downtown Dubai.

Every residence is oriented to the water. Interiors in warm stone and pale timber open onto deep terraces, while a podium of pools, gardens and social spaces keeps daily life unhurried and close to home.

Good to Know

Questions, answered.

Sapa sits on the east bank of Dubai Creek Harbour, overlooking the Ras Al Khor Wildlife Sanctuary — approximately fifteen minutes from Downtown Dubai and ten minutes from Dubai International Airport in off-peak conditions.

A collection of 132 one- to three-bedroom residences, including a limited series of creek-facing corner homes with wrap-around terraces on the upper floors.

A 60/40 plan: 20% on booking, 40% in staged instalments across construction, and the final 40% on completion. Final terms are set out in the Sales & Purchase Agreement.

Estimated completion is Q4 2028, with construction milestones published to registered buyers each quarter.

Yes — Dubai Creek Harbour is a designated freehold zone open to all nationalities, and residences above AED 2M qualify buyers to apply for the UAE 10-year Golden Visa.

Two podium levels host an infinity pool, fitness, yoga and boxing studios, kids' creative art room and play area, a gaming lounge, residents' lounge, landscaped gardens, sauna & steam, BBQ decks and 24/7 concierge.
